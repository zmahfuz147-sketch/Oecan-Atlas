WHY WE EXIST

Our purpose

OceanAtlas began as a practice project and grew into something I hope is useful to anyone curious about the ocean. It brings together climate indicators, marine species, regional profiles, and an interactive map in one place, designed to be clear, visual, and easy to explore.

BUILT ON RESEARCH

Our sources

The information on this site is gathered from reputable scientific and conservation sources, including NOAA, NASA, the IUCN Red List, FishBase, and the IPCC. Each fact was reviewed against these sources before publication. Many figures, such as temperatures, depths, and population estimates, are approximate and rounded for educational use.
